# RExchange

> Turn what you have into what you need.

RExchange is a campus-only exchange platform that helps students trade skills, resources, tickets, opportunities, and other useful things without using money.

Instead of treating an exchange as a simple one-to-one barter, RExchange looks at what students have, what they need, and what other students are looking for to help create meaningful exchanges.

## The Problem

Students constantly have things that other students need:

- A senior may have a calculator but need help with a project.
- A student may know Python but need help designing a presentation.
- Someone may have an event ticket they cannot use.
- Another student may be looking for exactly that ticket.

The problem is that these opportunities are scattered across WhatsApp groups, college communities, and personal networks.

More importantly, a student may have something valuable to offer but not know that anyone else wants it.

## The Solution

RExchange creates a structured campus exchange network.

Students can list:

**WHAT I HAVE**

What they can offer.

**WHAT I NEED**

What they are looking for.

RExchange then evaluates potential reciprocal exchanges while respecting the student's college and academic-year context.

## What Makes RExchange Different

### Make Me Matchable

A normal barter platform stops when it cannot find a direct match.

RExchange doesn't.

When a student doesn't have a direct reciprocal match, **Make Me Matchable** looks at the student's capabilities and the current demand on their campus.

For example:

I NEED
TI-84 Calculator

I COULD OFFER
Python Tutoring
